# Restriction data verification policy — draft

Each restriction requires geometry or road segment ID, restriction type and value, source name and URL/identifier, observation date, verification status, verifier and timestamp, and expiry/review date.

Statuses: `unverified`, `verified`, `disputed`, `expired`. Only `verified` and current evidence may support a positive truck-route safety decision, subject to coverage and legal constraints. Conflicting sources require review. Absence of restriction is NOT evidence that a road is suitable.

Official notices must come from authenticated official channels; user reports cannot be promoted to official notices. Maintain audit trail and source license checks. Establish verified sources with Israeli authorities before implementation; no official dataset is included here.
